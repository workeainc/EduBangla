## Dependency notes

- Phase 5F depends only on the accepted Phase 5E baseline; do not modify
  Phase 4 or the accepted Phase 5A–5E behaviours.
- Phase 5G Communication depends on the accepted identity, tenant, audit,
  student/enrollment and portal foundations. It has no Finance, Result or
  Promotion database dependency; later domains may consume it through narrow
  post-commit contracts.
- Phase 5H Timetable consumes existing AcademicYear, class, section, group,
  SubjectAssignment and TeacherAssignment foundations. It does not change
  attendance, examination schedules, finance, results, promotions or notices.
- A recurring billing/calendar feature is explicitly deferred. The Phase 5F
  charge model is explicit assignment and invoice generation, not a scheduler.

## Considered and rejected

- A separate `BillingPeriod` / recurring-invoice engine: no equivalent domain
  entity exists and the requested scope does not define recurrence rules.
  Adding one now would make financial meaning ambiguous.
- Teacher finance screens: the existing teacher capabilities are
  assignment-scoped academic work only; no business requirement justifies
  student financial visibility.
- Storing a client-calculated invoice balance as the authority: balances must
  be derived server-side from invoice items, posted adjustments and active
  allocations.
- Recurring finance, gateways, refunds, taxes and a ledger: explicitly
  deferred by the accepted Phase 5F boundary.
- Timetable, bulk import/export and teacher attendance: pilot "should have"
  work, not a stronger dependency candidate than notices.
- Analytics: requires stable, approved read-only domain outputs and remains a
  later governed foundation.
- Parent notice delivery: deferred because Guardian is not yet linked to an
  authenticated tenant membership.
- Communication delivery extensions: provider, queue, attachment, parent and
  automatic cross-domain delivery need their own decisions.
- Teacher attendance and timetable-driven attendance automation: deferred;
  the frozen attendance session/assignment model remains authoritative.
- Scheduling optimiser, rooms, substitutions and clash overrides: deferred;
  Plan 003 establishes only the recurring weekly academic timetable boundary.
