# Phase 5H — Academic Timetable Foundation

Phase 5H owns recurring weekly instructional timetable slots only. A timetable is scoped to one school, academic year, class and section; its sources are the existing teacher and subject assignments.

Included: draft editing, validated publication, archival, immutable publication snapshots, school-admin management, and scoped teacher/student read views.

Deferred: rooms or capacity, substitutions, calendar exceptions or holidays, attendance automation, examination scheduling, optimisation, bell schedules, imports/exports, printable output, notifications, APIs, and parent access.

Lifecycle: `draft → published → archived`. Published and archived records are historical and never edited in place.
