# Pilot Scope: One High School

## Must have

- School setup, academic years, classes, sections, groups, subjects, and teacher assignments.
- Student, guardian, teacher, and staff management; persistent student profiles and annual enrollment.
- Role-based portals for school administration, teacher, student, and parent.
- Manual daily attendance with reports.
- Exam setup, marks entry, configurable assessment/grade foundations, result publication, and report card.
- Online-exam foundation: schedule, server-side time window, attempts, autosave/submit design, and auditable answer capture.
- Notices and basic fee structures, payments, receipts, and due visibility.
- Tenant isolation, audit trail, backups/restore runbook, and critical calculation/authorization tests.

## Should have

- Bulk import/export with validation; timetable; question bank; teacher attendance; printable reports; queued notifications; SMS-provider adapter boundary.

## Future

- Biometric/QR attendance, payment gateways, advanced proctoring, library/transport/hostel, native mobile apps, central benchmarking, AI analytics, and government data exchange.

Pilot acceptance is real school use with authorized users, correct enrollment/attendance/results, recoverable operational data, and no cross-school data access.
